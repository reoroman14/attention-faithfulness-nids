\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data}
The datasets analysed in this study are publicly available from their original sources: LYCOS-IDS2017
\cite{rosay2021,rosay2022}, LycoS-Unicas-IDS2018 \cite{cantone2024}, and CIC-IDS2017 and CSE-CIC-IDS2018
\cite{sharafaldin2018}, the latter through AWS Open Data \cite{aws_cse_cic_ids2018}.
Per-flow rows and features are not redistributed.
The code, configuration files, summary result files and the scripts that generate every table, figure and number in
this article are in a GitHub repository \cite{repo_attn_nids}.
\repositoryStatus{}
Per-row intermediate files (parquet, npz and pkl) are not in the repository because of their size.
